Gotcha. This was a fake.

You just clicked a link in a simulated phishing email created for a school cybersecurity lesson. Nothing bad has happened — but if this had been a real attack, clicking could have been the first step to a compromised account.

Simulated attack by: STUDENT-NAME

What gave it away?

  • The sender's address didn't match the real organisation's domain.
  • It created false urgency ("your account will be closed in 24 hours").
  • The link text and the actual destination were different.
  • It asked you to log in or confirm details via a link, not the real site.

What to do with a real one

  • Don't click. Hover first and check where the link actually goes.
  • Go to the real site directly instead of using the email's link.
  • Report it to your IT team or use the "report phishing" button.
