What Is Phishing?

Phishing is when cybercriminals use fake emails, text messages, or websites that look legitimate to trick you into:

  • Revealing personal information like passwords or credit card details
  • Clicking dangerous links that install malware
  • Downloading harmful attachments
  • Sending money or gift cards to scammers

How to Protect Yourself

Remember these tips to stay safe online:

  • Think before you click! Carefully check links before clicking, especially in unexpected messages
  • Hover over links to see the actual URL destination before clicking
  • Look for spelling errors, unusual sender addresses, or strange formatting
  • Never provide personal information in response to an email or text
  • Use multi-factor authentication when available
  • Report suspicious messages to your school IT department
  • If you're not sure, ask a teacher or IT staff for help

Common Phishing Tactics Targeting Students

  • Grade scams: "Click here to view your updated grades"
  • Account verification: "Your student account will be deleted unless you verify now"
  • Financial aid: "You've qualified for a special scholarship - claim now"
  • Password reset: "Your school password has expired - update immediately"
  • Free stuff: "Claim your free laptop/gift card/discount as a student"

Remember: Your school will never ask for your password via email or text messages. Always go directly to the official school website by typing the address in your browser.
